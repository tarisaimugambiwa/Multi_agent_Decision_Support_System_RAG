# Research Questions for Empirical Paper
## Alera: AI-Powered Healthcare Decision Support System

**Date:** January 22, 2026  
**System:** Alera Healthcare Decision Support System  
**Focus:** RAG-Enhanced Multi-Agent AI for Clinical Decision Making

---

## 1. MAIN RESEARCH QUESTION

**How does a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)-enhanced multi-agent AI system improve diagnostic accuracy and clinical decision-making efficiency in resource-constrained healthcare settings?**

---

## 2. SPECIFIC RESEARCH QUESTIONS

### 2.1 System Effectiveness & Performance

**RQ1: Diagnostic Accuracy**
- What is the diagnostic accuracy of the AI-powered multi-agent system compared to traditional triage methods in primary healthcare settings?
- How does the confidence score generated by the system correlate with actual diagnostic accuracy?
- What is the concordance rate between AI-generated diagnoses and final doctor-confirmed diagnoses?

**RQ2: Clinical Decision Support**
- To what extent does the RAG-based knowledge base (14,179+ medical chunks) improve the relevance and evidence-based nature of diagnostic recommendations?
- How effectively does the system identify critical red flags and emergency conditions compared to manual triage?
- What is the false positive and false negative rate for urgent/critical case classification?

**RQ3: Workflow Efficiency**
- How much time does the AI system save nurses during the initial patient assessment and case creation process?
- What is the average time reduction in doctor case review when using AI-generated preliminary diagnoses?
- How does the system affect the overall patient throughput in outpatient settings?

### 2.2 Multi-Agent Architecture Evaluation

**RQ4: Agent Collaboration**
- How effectively do the three specialized agents (Coordinator, Diagnosis, Treatment) collaborate to produce comprehensive medical reports?
- What is the comparative performance of the multi-agent approach versus a single-agent AI system?
- Which agent contributes most significantly to diagnostic accuracy and treatment recommendation quality?

**RQ5: RAG Integration Impact**
- How does the integration of authoritative medical sources (WHO guidelines, ESPGHAN protocols, Uganda MoH standards) affect the quality of AI recommendations?
- What is the retrieval accuracy of relevant medical knowledge for different symptom categories?
- How does semantic search performance using FAISS and sentence-transformers impact response relevance?

### 2.3 User Adoption & Usability

**RQ6: Healthcare Professional Acceptance**
- What is the level of trust and acceptance of AI-generated diagnoses among nurses and doctors?
- How does role-based access (nurse vs. doctor dashboards) affect system usability and workflow integration?
- What are the perceived benefits and limitations of the system from healthcare workers' perspectives?

**RQ7: Clinical Decision Modification**
- How frequently do doctors modify or reject AI-generated diagnoses?
- What are the primary reasons for AI diagnosis rejection or modification?
- How does the system influence clinical decision-making patterns over time?

**RQ8: System Usability**
- What is the System Usability Scale (SUS) score for the Alera interface among different user groups?
- How effective is the image upload feature in supporting visual symptom documentation?
- What is the learning curve for nurses and doctors adopting the system?

### 2.4 Context-Specific Implementation

**RQ9: Resource-Constrained Settings**
- How suitable is the system for deployment in low-resource healthcare facilities with limited internet connectivity?
- What is the cost-effectiveness of implementing the Alera system compared to traditional diagnostic workflows?
- How does the system perform with varying levels of medical staff expertise?

**RQ10: Zimbabwe Healthcare Context**
- How well does the system adapt to the local context (Celsius/Kilograms standardization, regional disease patterns)?
- What is the system's performance in handling common pediatric and adult conditions prevalent in Zimbabwe?
- How effective is the system in supporting nurses with varying levels of clinical training?

### 2.5 Data Quality & System Reliability

**RQ11: Data Integrity**
- What is the completeness and accuracy of case data entered by nurses using the structured forms?
- How does the base64 image storage approach affect data integrity and accessibility?
- What is the audit trail compliance and data traceability throughout the case lifecycle?

**RQ12: System Reliability**
- What is the system uptime and availability during clinical operations?
- How does the system handle edge cases and incomplete patient information?
- What is the error rate in AI-generated reports and recommendations?

### 2.6 Patient Outcomes & Safety

**RQ13: Patient Safety**
- What is the rate of adverse events or missed diagnoses when using the AI system?
- How effectively does the notification system alert appropriate personnel for critical cases?
- What safety mechanisms prevent over-reliance on AI recommendations?

**RQ14: Patient Outcomes**
- How does the system impact time-to-treatment for patients?
- What is the rate of appropriate specialist referrals generated by the system?
- How does AI-assisted diagnosis affect patient satisfaction and perceived quality of care?

### 2.7 Knowledge Base & Continuous Learning

**RQ15: Medical Knowledge Coverage**
- How comprehensively does the current knowledge base (11 medical documents, 949,776 words) cover common presenting complaints?
- What knowledge gaps exist in the current RAG database for specific conditions or age groups?
- How frequently does the system fail to retrieve relevant medical information for given symptoms?

**RQ16: System Evolution**
- How does the system's diagnostic accuracy improve over time with case accumulation?
- What mechanisms exist for incorporating new medical guidelines and protocols?
- How effective is the feedback loop between doctor modifications and system learning?

## 3. HYPOTHESES

### H1: Diagnostic Performance
- **H1a:** The AI-powered multi-agent system will achieve >80% concordance with final doctor diagnoses.
- **H1b:** The system will demonstrate higher sensitivity for critical conditions compared to manual triage.
- **H1c:** RAG-enhanced recommendations will have higher clinical relevance scores compared to non-RAG AI systems.

### H2: Efficiency Gains
- **H2a:** Nurses using the system will complete initial patient assessments 30-40% faster than manual documentation.
- **H2b:** Doctors will spend 25-35% less time on preliminary case review with AI-generated reports.
- **H2c:** Overall patient waiting time will be reduced by at least 20% in facilities using the system.

### H3: User Acceptance
- **H3a:** Healthcare professionals will rate the system with a System Usability Scale (SUS) score >70.
- **H3b:** Trust in AI recommendations will increase with cumulative exposure and positive outcomes.
- **H3c:** Doctors will accept AI-generated diagnoses without modification in >60% of non-critical cases.

### H4: Multi-Agent Advantage
- **H4a:** The multi-agent architecture will produce more comprehensive reports than single-agent approaches.
- **H4b:** Treatment recommendations will be more evidence-based with RAG integration compared to purely rule-based systems.

### H5: Context Adaptation
- **H5a:** The system will perform equally well across different healthcare facility types (urban vs. rural).
- **H5b:** Local context standardization (units, regional diseases) will improve system acceptance and usability.

## 4. RESEARCH METHODOLOGY SUGGESTIONS

### 4.1 Study Design
- **Mixed Methods Approach:** Combine quantitative performance metrics with qualitative user experience data
- **Quasi-Experimental Design:** Pre-post comparison in pilot healthcare facilities
- **Controlled Trial:** Randomized assignment of patients to AI-assisted vs. traditional triage groups

### 4.2 Data Collection Methods

**Quantitative:**
- System log analysis (diagnostic accuracy, processing time, user interactions)
- Clinical audit data (concordance rates, diagnosis modifications, patient outcomes)
- Performance metrics (sensitivity, specificity, positive/negative predictive value)
- Efficiency metrics (time-to-diagnosis, throughput, resource utilization)

**Qualitative:**
- Semi-structured interviews with nurses and doctors
- Focus group discussions with healthcare workers
- Observation of clinical workflows
- User experience surveys (SUS, TAM - Technology Acceptance Model)

### 4.3 Evaluation Metrics

**System Performance:**
- Diagnostic accuracy (%)
- Confidence score calibration
- Red flag detection rate
- Knowledge retrieval relevance
- System response time

**Clinical Impact:**
- Time-to-treatment (minutes)
- Appropriate referral rate (%)
- Medication error rate
- Patient satisfaction scores
- Adverse event rate

**Operational Efficiency:**
- Average case processing time
- Patient throughput (patients/day)
- Staff workload reduction (%)
- Cost per patient encounter
- Return on investment (ROI)

**User Experience:**
- System Usability Scale (SUS) score
- Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) scores
- Training time required (hours)
- Error recovery success rate
- User satisfaction ratings

## 6. LIMITATIONS TO ADDRESS

### 6.1 Methodological Limitations
- Sample size constraints in pilot studies
- Selection bias in facility recruitment
- Temporal variations in patient acuity
- Inter-rater reliability in diagnosis assessment

### 6.2 System Limitations
- Dependency on quality of medical knowledge base
- Limited to documented symptoms and vital signs
- Potential for automation bias among clinicians
- Language and cultural adaptation challenges

### 6.3 Generalizability Concerns
- Context-specific to Zimbabwean healthcare system
- Limited to primary care settings
- Focus on common presenting complaints
- Requires validation in diverse healthcare environments
