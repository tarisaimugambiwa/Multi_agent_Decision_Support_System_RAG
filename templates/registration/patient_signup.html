Your Patient Rights

By creating an account with Alera Health System, you are entitled to the following rights:

  • Privacy & Confidentiality: Your medical information is protected and will only be shared with your healthcare providers.
  • Access to Records: You have the right to view and request copies of your medical records.
  • Informed Consent: You will be informed about your diagnosis, treatment options, and potential risks.
  • Quality Care: You have the right to receive safe, competent, and respectful healthcare services.
  • Data Security: Your personal and medical data is encrypted and stored securely.
  • Non-Discrimination: You will receive care without discrimination based on age, gender, religion, or medical condition.
By signing up, you acknowledge that you have read and understood your patient rights.
